# Play Music on Web and Native

Start music from an album, artist, or track, then keep browsing your library. The player holds the current track and queue while you move between pages.

## Start with an album

1. Open **Albums** and choose an album with imported audio files.
2. Choose **Play** to follow its track order, or **Shuffle** for a shuffled queue.
3. Open the current player to see the track, artwork, progress, and transport controls.

If the album is still **Wanted**, it needs an imported playable file first. For files that do not appear as expected, check the [music folder and metadata rules](../library/audio.md).

## In the web app

The persistent player stays available while you browse. Open **Up Next** to see the queue and choose another track. Shuffle creates a fixed order, so the list you see is the order that will play.

Use the timeline or waveform to seek, and the transport controls to pause or change tracks. You can minimize the player to a compact bubble, move it to a screen edge, and tap its artwork to restore it.

The browser can expose track information and playback controls to the operating system through its media controls. Which controls appear depends on the browser and device.

## In the native app

Tap the mini player to open **Now Playing**. The full player includes artwork, a seek control, playback transport, shuffle, and repeat. Open **Show Queue** to inspect **Up Next**, then choose **Show Now Playing** to return to the artwork and player.

On iPhone and iPad, **Choose Audio Output** opens the system output picker. Available outputs depend on the device and connected audio routes. System Now Playing controls can also show the current title and artwork outside the app.

Dismiss the full player to browse while playback continues. Audiobook queues preserve their source order; music shuffle controls are not a promise that chapters will play randomly.

## Resume on another device

Use the same server and account to share saved playback progress and play history. A saved position is different from a live device handoff: opening another client does not transfer its current queue or make it a remote control for the first player.

For audiobook parts, chapter alignment, and reading alongside playback, use the [read-and-listen guide](./read-and-listen.md). For installation and connection, see [Connect the Native App](./native-apps.md).
