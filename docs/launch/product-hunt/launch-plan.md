## Campaign principles

1. Ask for experience and feedback, never votes.
2. Lead with the problem: one media collection is forced through many disconnected
   products.
3. Prove the breadth quickly: request, manage, watch, listen, read, and browse.
4. Make native playback, the reader, and Apple TV concrete rather than treating them
   as platform logos.
5. Route curious users to the public site first and ready testers directly to
   TestFlight.
6. Respond as the maker. Do not automate Product Hunt conversations.

## Copy bank

### Product Hunt forum

**Why does self-hosted media still feel like five separate products?**

My media is already one collection, but using it means crossing a chain of unrelated
apps: discovery and requests, acquisition, metadata, files, then playback or reading.
Each handoff repeats identity, setup, state, and troubleshooting.

I’m building Prismedia around the opposite idea: the movie, book, album, or audiobook
stays the same item from the moment someone wants it through acquisition and finally
watching, listening, or reading it. The server and web workspace are self-hosted, with
native iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV experiences on top.

Before launch, I would love concrete feedback from people who run their own media
stack:

- Which handoff loses the most context today?
- Would a unified operations workspace or a better native consumption experience
  matter more to you?
- What would an app need to prove before you trusted it with more of the lifecycle?

## Measurement

Capture these at launch, 24 hours, seven days, and 14 days:

- Product Hunt visits, comments, reviews, and followers
- Public-site sessions from Product Hunt
- TestFlight joins and meaningful tester feedback
- Install-guide visits
- GitHub stars, issues, and discussions attributable to the launch
- Repeated questions, objections, and requested media experiences

The campaign succeeds if it finds engaged self-hosters and produces clear product
learning. Product Hunt rank is useful distribution, not the product goal.

## After launch

- Convert repeated questions into documentation.
- Request reviews only after someone has had a useful experience.
- Publish a short “what we learned” note with concrete changes.
- Revisit Product Hunt’s
  [post-launch guidance](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/days-after-launch) for the
