## Video direction

- Palette system: true black (`ink-black`) is the default ground; primary type is
  `cream`, supporting type is `cream-muted`, and authored framing uses
  `border-dark`, `surface-1`, and `surface-2`. The eight `prism-*` colors appear
  only as the literal white-light-to-spectrum identity, a selected rail, or one
  decisive handoff. Silver reversal is rare; there is no ambient rainbow wash.
- Type system: Geist Sans display and body create the editorial hierarchy;
  JetBrains Mono is reserved for compact technical chrome. Product screenshots
  remain visually authentic and are never repainted to match the title cards.
- Motion grammar: every move uses a smooth long-tail settle (`power3` by
  default), deterministic `fromTo` entrances, transform/paint-only motion, and
  velocity-matched internal seams. This is a silent film, so the on-screen copy
  is the cue rail: reveal one thought at a time, distribute the meaningful
  reveals through the back half, and allow at least one clean reading beat
  before the next thought arrives. Never front-load the canvas.
- Rhythm and holds: Frames 4, 5, and 10 are deliberate breathers. Frame 4 holds
  the paused Apple TV playback chrome; Frame 5 holds both the Literary Serif
  reader and its customization panel; Frame 10 holds the two closing actions.
  Settled content is still, with at most one finite low-amplitude jitter on the
  focal mark. No late camera drift or lazy breathing.
- Layout invariant: keep every load-bearing element in the top ~83% of frame,
  preserving the bottom 17% keep-out even though captions are disabled. Each
  shot has one dominant focal surface, a 3:1 hierarchy, and authored depth from
  overlap, scale, or atmosphere rather than generic drop shadows.
- Negative list: no slideshow front-load-and-freeze; no screensaver field of
  independently floating cards; no bouncy or elastic default easing; no fake
  cursor; no browser chrome; no generic purple/blue AI gradient; no particle
  fog; no perpetual loops; no off-brand neon; no UI hidden while paused; no
  important copy or controls in the bottom keep-out.
