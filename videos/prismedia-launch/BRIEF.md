## Intent

Create a polished, silent launch film that makes Prismedia understandable
without requiring narration. Begin with fragmented self-hosted media needs,
then show how one private library carries discovery, requests, acquisition,
identity, files, playback, reading, listening, and household progress across
web, iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

The film should feel calm, cinematic, technically credible, and native to the
existing Prismedia design language. It markets the product rather than touring
every page. Real product proof must do most of the persuasion.

## Customizations

- Produce a truly silent master: no narration, music, ambient audio, or sound effects.
- Use concise on-screen lines as chapter-level guidance, not constant captions.
- Keep the reader settings legible long enough to see Dark, Literary Serif,
  120% text size, 105% weight, and 1.7 line spacing.
- Hold the paused Apple TV frame long enough to read title, Direct Play,
  resolution, codecs, timeline, audio, subtitles, and speed controls.
- Preserve a center-safe action region so later 30-second, 15-second, square,
  and vertical cutdowns can reuse the master.

## Notes

- The canonical story and timing guide is
  `../../docs/launch/media-production-plan.md`, section
  “72-second product-film storyboard.”
- Use true black and opaque neutral surfaces as the ground. Glass belongs only
  on navigation, floating labels, controls, and transitions.
- The prism spectrum is identity, not decoration: reserve emitted light for
  the opening, major medium changes, and closing.
- No generic SaaS gradients, neon bloom, pill-heavy cards, fake dashboards,
  invented UI, competitor logos, or claims that Prismedia requires no external
  services.
- Use “source available,” “self-hosted,” and “the services you choose.”
- Treat third-party artwork and playback frames as launch proof pending the
  publication-rights review recorded in the media asset manifest.
