# Apple review library credits

This server contains a small demonstration library for Apple App Review. It
does not contain household media.

## Video

**Big Buck Bunny** © 2008 Blender Foundation. Licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0. <https://peach.blender.org/>

**Elephants Dream** and **Caminandes: Gran Dillama** are Blender Foundation open
movie works distributed through Blender's official demo-media archive. The
files are used unmodified and retain their embedded credits.
<https://download.blender.org/demo/movies/>

## Book and audiobook

**Alice's Adventures in Wonderland** by Lewis Carroll, illustrated by John

<https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/lewis-carroll/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/john-tenniel>

Audiobook read by Eric Leach for LibriVox. LibriVox recordings are dedicated to
